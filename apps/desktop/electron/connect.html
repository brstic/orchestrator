Which office do you want to walk into?

Connect to an office

An office runs on a server and stays open; you walk in from here. Type its address and ask to be let in — or paste an invitation you were sent. While you are in, your own computer has a room there for the sessions you run on it.

They see this code next to your name. Tell it to them if they ask whether it is really you.
An invitation names the exact computer to trust. An address is asked only if its certificate is one this computer trusts.

An office on this computer

An office of your own that runs here and only while this app is open: your employees, your projects.

With the server’s own owner token (on the server’s machine or through a tunnel)
The token is in ~/.orchestrator/server/owner-token on the machine that runs the server. It is stored on this computer, encrypted by the operating system where that is available.
